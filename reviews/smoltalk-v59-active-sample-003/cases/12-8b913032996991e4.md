<pre>
Data visualization plays a crucial role in understanding a data set by providing a clear and concise representation of the data, making it easier to identify patterns, trends, and correlations. Visualization helps to transform complex data into a more digestible and meaningful format, allowing users to quickly grasp the essence of the data.

Through data visualization, you can:

Identify patterns and trends that might be difficult to detect in raw data, such as outliers, clusters, or relationships between variables.
Communicate insights and findings more effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences, facilitating collaboration and decision-making.
Facilitate exploratory data analysis, enabling you to formulate hypotheses and refine your investigation.

### Turn 6 - ASSISTANT

<pre>
A relatively simple type of plot used to determine the relationship or correlation between two variables is a Scatter Plot. It displays the values of two variables as points on a grid, with one variable on the x-axis and the other on the y-axis. The pattern of the points can indicate the strength and direction of the relationship between the two variables, such as a positive or negative correlation, or no correlation at all.
