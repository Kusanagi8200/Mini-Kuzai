</pre>

### Turn 2 - ASSISTANT

<pre>
To find the average score, we add up the scores from all four exams and divide by the number of exams.&#32;

The total score is 71 + 82 + 91 + 78 = 322 points.&#32;

Dividing the total score by the number of exams, we get 322 / 4 = 80.5 points.&#32;

So, the student&#x27;s average score is 80.5 points.
</pre>

### Turn 3 - USER

<pre>
A shoe factory produces a certain style of shoe in two colors, brown and black. Each day, the factory&#x27;s production is such that it produces 150 pairs more black shoes than brown shoes. If, on a given day, the factory produces 800 brown shoes, how many black shoes are produced that day?
</pre>

### Turn 4 - ASSISTANT

<pre>
We know that the factory produces 150 more pairs of black shoes than brown shoes each day. On the given day, the factory produces 800 brown shoes.&#32;

To find the number of black shoes produced, we simply add 150 to the number of brown shoes.&#32;

800 (brown shoes) + 150 = 950 black shoes.

So, the factory produces 950 black shoes that day.
</pre>

### Turn 5 - USER

<pre>
What are the total number of shoes produced that day?
</pre>

### Turn 6 - ASSISTANT

<pre>
We know that the factory produced 800 brown shoes and 950 black shoes on that day.&#32;

To find the total number of shoes produced, we add the number of brown shoes to the number of black shoes.&#32;

800 (brown shoes) + 950 (black shoes) = 1750 shoes.

So, the total number of shoes produced that day is 1750.
</pre>
