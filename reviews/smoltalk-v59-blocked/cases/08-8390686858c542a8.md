<pre>
The `PercentDifference()` method in the `Word` class calculates the percentage difference between the number of syllables in the Korean word and the number of syllables in the English translation. It is calculated as follows:

`percent = ((|syllablesKorean - syllablesEnglish|) / Max(syllablesKorean, syllablesEnglish)) * 100`

where `|syllablesKorean - syllablesEnglish|` is the absolute value of the difference between the number of syllables in the Korean word and the number of syllables in the English translation, and `Max(syllablesKorean, syllablesEnglish)` is the larger of the two values.

The purpose of this method is to provide a measure of how different the Korean word and the English translation are in terms of syllable count. It can be useful in determining the difficulty of learning the word for English speakers or in identifying potential errors in translations.

One potential issue with the calculation method is that it does not take into account the actual difference in pronunciation between the two words. For example, the word &quot;고양이&quot; (cat) has three syllables in Korean and one syllable in English, but the pronunciation of the two words is quite different. Another potential issue is that syllable count may not be a good indicator of overall word complexity or difficulty.
