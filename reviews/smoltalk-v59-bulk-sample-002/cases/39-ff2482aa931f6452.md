<pre>
Calculating the average sale price is a straightforward process. You&#x27;ll need to divide the total revenue by the number of sales.

To do this, sum up the prices of all sales to get the total revenue. Then, count the number of sales you&#x27;ve made. Finally, divide the total revenue by the number of sales. This will give you the average sale price.

For example, if your total revenue is $100,000 and you&#x27;ve made 200 sales, the average sale price would be $500 ($100,000 ÷ 200 sales).

You can also calculate the average sale price for specific products or time periods by filtering your data accordingly. This can help you understand how the average sale price changes over time or varies between products.

Keep in mind that if you have a wide range of prices, the average sale price might not perfectly represent the typical sale. In such cases, you might also consider calculating the median sale price, which is the middle value of all sale prices when arranged in order. However, for most purposes, the average sale price should provide a good starting point.
