Before addressing the issue directly with your colleague, take some time to reflect on the situation. Perhaps their approach has helped catch errors or improve processes that you haven&#x27;t noticed.

By framing the conversation in this way, you&#x27;re acknowledging the value of their approach while also expressing your concerns about the impact on productivity. This may help your colleague understand your perspective and be more willing to find a middle ground. Additionally, try to focus on specific examples or instances where you felt the excessive attention to detail slowed things down, rather than making general statements. This will help to keep the conversation concrete and focused on finding solutions rather than placing blame.

<pre>
