Handling RSVPs efficiently is crucial to get an accurate headcount for food, drinks, and arrangements. Here&#x27;s a simple plan to manage RSVPs for your sister&#x27;s party:

Once the RSVP deadline has passed, finalize your guest list and provide the headcount to your caterer, bartender, or other vendors. Make sure to have a few extra seats and supplies, just in case some guests bring unexpected plus-ones or there are last-minute changes.

By having a clear plan for handling RSVPs, you&#x27;ll be able to get an accurate headcount, avoid last-minute stress, and ensure that your sister&#x27;s party is a success.
