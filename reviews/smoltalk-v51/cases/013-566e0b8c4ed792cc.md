<pre>
To determine if option 1 is cheaper, you need to calculate the cost per person for that option. Since the room costs $300 per night regardless of the number of occupants, you would divide the total room cost by the number of people staying, which is 5 in this case.

Dividing $300 by 5 gives you $60 per person per night. Interestingly, this is the same cost as option 2, which charges $60 per person per night. This means that in your specific situation, with 5 people traveling together, both options would cost the same amount per night.
