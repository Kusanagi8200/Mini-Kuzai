# Mini-Kuzai V50 - cognitive and creative pilot review 001

Status: **8 public-external SmolTalk examples re-examined under V50 criteria**.
This is a methodology pilot, not the full 1,139-record V44 audit and not
an independent evaluation or authorization for training.

This pilot deliberately includes previously reviewed V48/V49 cases to
distinguish **behavioral usefulness** from the old all-or-nothing
conversation-quality judgment. V49 status fields and full original
public-source text remain **untouched** and auditable.

Review categories:
- A = relevant coherent candidate for further verification;
- B = potentially valuable after segmentation, repair or other scoped
  human review, **not usable unmodified as a full positive dialogue**;
- C = low-priority or unreliable for the current persona objective,
  irrespective of any narrower technical usefulness.

## Preliminary lesson

Three of the eight pilot cases are strong **A** candidates.
Three cases previously rejected are **B** under the new lens because
creative value may survive controlled segmentation. Two are **C** for
positive persona training in their current complete form.

These numbers are **not representative acceptance rates** and must not
be extrapolated to V44, the 8,000-source pool, or the full Phase 03 mix.

Being behaviorally interesting never makes an incorrect assistant
response automatically correct. Training-target repair must be
explicitly authored, verified and recorded separately. No V49 decision
is overwritten and no new training file is created.
