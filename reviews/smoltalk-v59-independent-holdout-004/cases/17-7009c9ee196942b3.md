The best plot is often a delicate balance between the two. This technique is often referred to as &quot;hindsight inevitability.&quot;

A plot that&#x27;s too predictable can feel formulaic and lack tension, while one that&#x27;s too unexpected can feel arbitrary and unsatisfying. A great example of this is the Harry Potter series by J.K. Rowling. The reveal of Snape&#x27;s true loyalties is both surprising and, in retrospect, entirely consistent with his character and actions throughout the series. Rowling expertly wove subtle hints and clues that made the twist feel both unexpected and inevitable.

In your own writing, try to find a balance between laying subtle hints and avoiding obvious giveaways. Use foreshadowing, symbolism, and character inconsistencies to create a sense of tension and mystery, making the ultimate reveal feel both surprising and satisfying. By doing so, you&#x27;ll craft a plot that will keep your readers engaged, invested, and eager to re-read your story to spot the clues they missed the first time around.
