# Mini-Kuzai Phase 03 - V48 public SmolTalk review results

Status: first qualitative pass completed on the public external SmolTalk sample.
Source: [SmolTalk V48 review index](README.md).

## Human-style case assessment

All 68 GitHub review case files were individually examined and assigned a
reasoned decision. The decisions are quality-screening recommendations based
on the public SmolTalk source conversations; they are **not** automatic
authorization to introduce records into a model TRAIN split.

| Queue group | RETAIN | REJECT | HOLD | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| RELABEL | 5 | 5 | 2 | 12 |
| RESCUE | 8 | 12 | 4 | 24 |
| BASE | 10 | 14 | 8 | 32 |
| **Total** | **23** | **31** | **14** | **68** |

Every case has its own Markdown record under [cases/](cases/) with a status,
a textual justification and the original **public external** source
conversation. No private ChatGPT corpus or private-source data is involved.

## Lessons from the first pass

- Some examples maintain topic and user corrections across all three exchanges;
  others concatenate unrelated requests, so lexical continuity cannot alone
  demonstrate conversational quality.
- Mathematical or programming examples were rejected when their worked
  methods or output contracts were incorrect, even if some final numbers or
  simple demonstrations happened to look plausible.
- Creative examples show meaningful variation in context retention,
  roleplaying, narrative coherence and responsiveness to revision.
- HOLD denotes unresolved quality questions such as factual checking,
  unsupported claims, ambiguous requirements, or a questionable explanation
  despite an otherwise useful interaction.

These 68 cases were deliberately sampled for diagnostic review. Their
acceptance rate is **not** a statistically validated acceptance rate for the
full 8,000-record reservoir or the 1,139-record provisional V44 selection.
