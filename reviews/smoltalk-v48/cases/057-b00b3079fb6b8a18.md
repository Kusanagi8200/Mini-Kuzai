A long term care facility is looking at its data on falls in the facility. If their annual report shows 150 total falls with 50 of those falls resulting in some type of injury and 5 of those falls resulting in a serious injury that required a hospital visit, what is the overall percentage of falls that resulted in an injury requiring a hospital visit in that year?
</pre>

### Turn 2 - ASSISTANT

So, the calculation would be: (5 / 150) * 100 = 3.33%

This means that approximately 3.33% of the falls in the facility that year resulted in a serious injury requiring a hospital visit.
</pre>
