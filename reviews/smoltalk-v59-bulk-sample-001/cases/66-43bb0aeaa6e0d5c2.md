Consider a dataset of exam scores with 20 students and the mean is 75, and the standard deviation is 5.  Convert these exam scores from z-scores back to raw scores for the following z-scores.  z-score of -2, z-score of 0, and z-score of 1.5.
</pre>

### Turn 2 - ASSISTANT

<pre>
To convert these z-scores back to raw scores, we can use the formula for converting z-scores to raw scores:&#32;

Raw score = Mean + (z-score * Standard Deviation)

Let&#x27;s calculate the raw scores for the given z-scores:

For a z-score of -2:
Raw score = 75 + (-2 * 5)
Raw score = 75 - 10
Raw score = 65

For a z-score of 0:
Raw score = 75 + (0 * 5)
Raw score = 75

For a z-score of 1.5:
Raw score = 75 + (1.5 * 5)
Raw score = 75 + 7.5
Raw score = 82.5

So, the corresponding raw scores are 65, 75, and 82.5 for the given z-scores.
</pre>

### Turn 6 - ASSISTANT

<pre>
To calculate a 95% confidence interval for the average salary, we can use the formula:

CI = Sample Mean ± (Critical Value * (Sample Standard Deviation / √Sample Size))

For a 95% confidence interval, the critical value is typically 1.96 for large samples, but since we have a sample size of 20, which is relatively small, we should use the t-distribution. The degrees of freedom for the t-distribution is n-1, which is 20-1 = 19. The critical value for a 95% confidence interval with 19 degrees of freedom is approximately 2.093.

Plugging in the values, we get:

CI = $75,000 ± (2.093 * ($5,000 / √20))
CI = $75,000 ± (2.093 * ($5,000 / 4.472))
CI = $75,000 ± (2.093 * $1,118.35)
CI = $75,000 ± $2,340.52

Lower bound: $75,000 - $2,340.52 = $72,659.48
Upper bound: $75,000 + $2,340.52 = $77,340.52

So, the 95% confidence interval for the average salary is approximately ($72,659.48, $77,340.52).
