## Case-by-case repair specification

### V51-009 - business writing and corporate relationship

**Useful behavior**

The dialogue maintains a coherent professional-writing objective while moving from a formal proposal opening to corporate-relationship wording and then to environmental-remediation positioning.

**Defects**

- "Wholly owned subsidiary" is accepted without evidence that the parent owns 100 percent of the subsidiary.
- Corporate leadership, sustainability and resource-access claims are repeated as facts even though they were supplied only as proposed wording.
- The full six-turn record combines three related but separable writing tasks.

**Repair plan**

1. Segment the formal-letter opening as an independent writing/editing example.
2. For the ownership terminology fragment, make the answer conditional: use "wholly owned" only if 100 percent ownership has been verified; otherwise use a neutral parent/subsidiary formulation.
3. For the strategic-partnership fragment, preserve the writing objective but mark leadership, environmental credentials, access to parent resources and shared capabilities as claims that must be verified before assertion.
4. Prefer language such as "if accurate" or "if this relationship is documented" rather than endorsing supplied claims.

**Future derivative status:** candidate only after segmented targets are independently reviewed.

### V51-012 - retail treasure-hunt ideation

**Useful behavior**

The assistant sustains the same retail concept across layout, product rotation and promotional language, with useful creative continuity.

**Defects**

- Specific rotation frequencies are presented as broadly applicable despite lacking store-specific evidence.
- Scarcity and exclusivity language can become misleading if inventory or access is not genuinely limited.
- Recommendations are framed too prescriptively rather than as hypotheses to test.

**Repair plan**

1. Keep the multi-turn structure because it demonstrates continuity.
2. Replace fixed universal rotation schedules with testable starting hypotheses tied to store type, inventory turnover and observed customer response.
3. Add measurement: repeat visits, conversion, sell-through, basket size and customer feedback should determine cadence.
4. Permit scarcity or exclusivity language only when it is factually true.
5. Preserve the adventure/discovery framing while removing deceptive pressure tactics.

**Future derivative status:** candidate after uncertainty and truthfulness calibration.

### V51-013 - hotel pricing comparison

**Useful behavior**

The conversation correctly develops the crossover logic from five travelers to a larger group and then to a solo traveler.

**Defects**

- The final assistant sentence is internally awkward and contradictory before eventually giving the correct conclusion.
- The "larger group is always better" reasoning implicitly assumes one room can legally and practically hold the whole group.

**Repair plan**

1. Preserve the full multi-turn reasoning sequence.
2. Rewrite the final solo-traveler answer directly: option 2 costs 60 dollars versus 300 dollars for option 1.
3. State the key assumption for larger groups: the fixed-price room must actually accommodate the entire group under the property's occupancy rules.
4. Keep the five-person break-even point only under that stated assumption.

**Future derivative status:** candidate after minor target cleanup and explicit constraint statement.

### V52-003 - editing tasks with topic discontinuity

**Useful behavior**

Turns 3-6 form a strong local revision chain: the user requests more vivid prose and then provides precise concision edits, which the assistant follows.

**Defects**

- Turns 1-2 concern an unrelated seafloor-spreading paragraph and should not be treated as contextual evidence for turns 3-6.
- The seafloor paragraph contains a scientific statement about magnetic properties that requires factual correction or verification before reuse.

**Repair plan**

1. Segment turns 3-6 as the primary behavioral candidate because they demonstrate iterative editing and response to user feedback.
2. Do not include turns 1-2 in that derivative.
3. If turns 1-2 are ever reused separately, independently verify and correct the geology claim before creating a positive target.
4. Preserve the final concise earthquake rewrite only within its own editing context.

### V52-012 - monthly sales and seasonality

**Useful behavior**

The assistant tracks a developing analytical question and incorporates new business context when the user reveals that the company sells toys.

**Defects**

- The first answer identifies the wrong periods as the largest percentage increases.
- From the supplied values, March to April is about 13.6 percent and April to May is 12 percent; July to August is about 9.4 percent and November to December about 7.1 percent.
- One monotonically increasing year is insufficient to establish seasonality.
- The toy-business context makes a holiday effect plausible but does not prove that the observed December level is caused by seasonality.

**Repair plan**

1. Recompute the monthly changes correctly.
2. Keep the distinction between long-term trend and seasonality explicit.
3. State that repeated annual cycles are needed to identify a seasonal pattern reliably.
4. Treat toy-industry context as a hypothesis generator, not proof about this particular series.
5. Recommend multi-year data and, when available, decomposition or year-over-year comparison.

**Future derivative status:** candidate after numerical correction and uncertainty calibration.

### V52-013 - malicious-wish hypothetical

**Useful behavior**

This is a strong imaginative reasoning sequence. The assistant follows newly introduced rules, reasons about a self-referential wish and responds to a terminology clarification.

**Defect**

The later answers overstate the logic: the fact that the being may only add words does not by itself prove every harmful addition is self-contradictory or that the wish is guaranteed safe.

**Repair plan**

1. Preserve the full hypothetical and the user's clarification of "self-referential".
2. Replace the guarantee of safety with conditional reasoning.
3. Explicitly distinguish possible rule interpretations: whether additions occur before the wish takes effect, how scope is resolved, and whether added exceptions can alter the effective condition.
4. Conclude that safety depends on the fictional semantics; self-reference alone does not establish a proof of immunity.
5. Preserve the assistant's willingness to refine terminology after user feedback.

**Future derivative status:** high-value candidate after logical calibration.

### V52-025 - temperature trend and regression

**Useful behavior**

The conversation progresses naturally from descriptive analysis to correlation/regression and then to model-choice reasoning.

**Defect**

The stated mean is wrong. The twelve temperatures sum to 816 F, giving an exact mean of **68.0 F**, not approximately 69 F.

**Repair plan**

1. Correct the mean to 68.0 F.
2. Preserve the line-graph, correlation, slope and residual-analysis discussion.
3. Avoid calling individual deviations "anomalies" without a defined criterion.
4. Present linear regression as a reasonable first model to test rather than as automatically validated.
5. Retain the recommendation to inspect residuals before accepting the model.

**Future derivative status:** candidate after direct numeric correction.
